# Organic / hand-drawn

## Summary
Sketchy, wobbly lines, hand-drawn icons, paper texture, warm.

## Traits
- Sketchy lines
- Hand-drawn icons
- Paper texture
- Warm colors
- Imperfect, human feel

## CSS
```css
border: 2px dashed #333;
font-family: 'Caveat', cursive;
```

## When to use
Personal, creative, warm brands

## When to avoid
Precision-required (finance/medical)
